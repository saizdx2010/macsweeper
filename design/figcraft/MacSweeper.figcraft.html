02

Icon set: "Sweep Line"

41 custom icons replace the duplicated SF Symbols (14 categories currently share internaldrive).

  • Grid: 24 × 24, 2 px live-area padding (red dashed), 1.6 stroke, round caps and joins.
  • Shapes: rounded rects r 2–3, no hairline details below 1.5 px, so they read at 16 px.
  • Fill: line only. Filled dots are reserved for eyes, buttons and indicators.
  • Color: monochrome templates in code. Category color comes from a 14 % tint tile (direction A/B).
  • Export: icons/*.svg → Xcode asset catalog, "Preserve Vector Data", rendered as template.
